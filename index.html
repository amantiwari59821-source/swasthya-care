Clinical Best Practices

Critical Medication Safety Rules

Evidence-based clinical guidelines that prevent complications and accidental overdoses.

Fever in Pregnancy

Paracetamol is the gold standard antipyretic in pregnancy. NSAIDs (Ibuprofen, Aspirin) must be avoided, especially in the 3rd trimester due to risks to fetal circulation and amniotic fluid.

Never Give Aspirin to Kids

Aspirin in patients under 16 years can cause Reye's Syndrome, a life-threatening swelling of the brain and liver. Always use Paracetamol instead.

Check Bottle Strength (120 vs 250)

Paracetamol syrups exist in 120mg/5ml (Junior) and 250mg/5ml (Forte DS). DS has more than double the active drug. Always check bottle strength before measuring!

Use Calibrated Syringes/Caps

Kitchen spoons range wildly between 3 ml and 8 ml. Always use the calibrated measuring cup or oral syringe provided in the medicine package.
